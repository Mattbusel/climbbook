# App Review notes

---

No account, login or network connection is required.

PRIVACY: no data is collected. Everything is stored on the device in the app's Documents folder. No account, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

HOW TO USE: The Wall tab shows the best send this month, a grade progression chart (hardest climb topped each week), monthly stats and records. The red + button in the tab bar logs a climb: pick a grade (holds), how it went (Flash, Send, Project, Repeat), the number of attempts, the place and tape colour, then "Log it". Sessions lists every day of climbing grouped by place; tap one to see its climbs and add notes. Pyramid stacks climbs by grade. Gear picks the grading system (V scale or Font) and holds export and Pro.

PHOTOS: the camera is used only when the user taps Camera while logging a climb (Pro). The system photo picker is used for the library, which needs no permission.

IN-APP PURCHASE: the app is free. There is one non-consumable purchase, Climbbook Pro (com.mattbusel.climbbook.pro). Free: unlimited logging, sessions from the last 60 days, records, 12-week progression, this month's pyramid. Pro: full history, 6-month and 1-year progression, the pyramid over longer ranges with style and flash-rate breakdowns, photos, custom grading systems and CSV export. To see the paywall: tap 6M or 1Y on the progression chart, or "3 months" on the Pyramid tab, or "Your gym's own system" on the Gear tab, or See Pro on the Climbbook Pro card at the bottom of Gear. Restore purchase is on the paywall and on that card.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
A training log for indoor and outdoor boulderers: record each climb's grade, result and attempts and see progress over time. General audience; rated 4+.

3. SETUP AND ACCESS
No setup, login or credentials. A new install starts empty.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS AND PLATFORMS
None. No network requests, analytics, advertising or third-party frameworks. Built with SwiftUI, PhotosUI and StoreKit 2.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. All art, text and code are my own work. Place names in the demo are fictional or public geographic names.
